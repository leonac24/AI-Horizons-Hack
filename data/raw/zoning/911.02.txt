Base Zoning Districts. As Base Zoning District Regulations, all uses may be subject to additional regulations imposed by applicable Overlay Zoning Districts.

[CAPTURE NOTE] Only the residential rows relevant to housing typologies are captured here. The table was flattened: each district cell is written as DISTRICT=VALUE with the cell text unchanged; "-" means the cell is blank (use not listed as allowed). Column groups: Residential: R1D R1A R2 R3 RM; Mixed Use: NDO LNC NDI UNC HC GI UI UC-MU UC-E R-MU; Special: P H EMI; DT (Downtown) subcolumns: DT-GT DT-(unnamed); RIV (Riverfront) subcolumns: RIV-RM RIV-MU RIV-NS RIV-GI RIV-IMU. Residential district codes on the zoning map combine a Use Subdistrict (R1D, R1A, R2, R3, RM) with a Development Subdistrict (VL, L, M, H, VH), e.g. R1D-H or RM-M; the use column applies to every density suffix.
